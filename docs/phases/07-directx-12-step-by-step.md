The site's side of stages 27e and 27f in CF Gaming's plan. Written on 13 November 2026 after Marc's "Yes, write them". Agree it with Marc before it starts.

## The aim

Before you start explains, step by step, what someone does to play DirectX 12 games once the public download no longer carries Apple's D3DMetal. It also says what CF Gaming does for them by itself.

## What it waits for

- **27e in the app:** **Add DirectX 12…**, and its page in the app's guide.

The plan's first question (which builds carry D3DMetal) decides what the site says.

## The steps

1. **Before you start,** in the app's own words from its guide page:
   - the free Apple Account;
   - Apple's Game Porting Toolkit download, which CF Gaming opens in the person's own browser (it never asks for or sees their Apple password);
   - what CF Gaming does by itself once the disk image is in Downloads.
2. **Home, About and Licences and source:** changed where they say D3DMetal comes with the app. Licences says D3DMetal is Apple's, downloaded by each person under their own licence from Apple.
3. **Apple's downloads page** linked by its own address, checked then. No Apple logos.
4. **Pictures of the steps,** if 27b exports them, through phase 04's way of showing them.
