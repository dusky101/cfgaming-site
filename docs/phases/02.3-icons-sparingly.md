# 02.3: icons, sparingly

Fixes after Marc's look at the site, 14 November 2026. Agree it with Marc before it starts.

## Why, in Marc's words

"Also remove the icons from the start of every line. if ever there was a way to tell if a website was built by ai that is it.. 🙂 Ai loves the icons..." Then: "use icons still but sparingly."

## What's there now (counted 14 November)

- **A tile before every section heading:** 32 across the pages (`.with-tile`), and 4 more on the DirectX 12 draft.
- **A tile at the top of every page,** in `PageHeader.astro`: 7.
- **An icon on each "where next" card,** in `NextSteps.astro`.
- **A gradient mark before every line of every list** (`.ticks li::before` in `global.css`): the marks at "the start of every line".
- **The download icon** on the download buttons.

## The proposal

- **Kept:**
  - **the download icon** on the download buttons: it says what the button does;
  - **one icon for each of Home's three highlights** (Windows games, consoles, Custom Setup), until phase 04's pictures take their place;
  - **Install's numbered steps** (1 to 4): they're numbers, not icons.
- **Gone:**
  - the tile before every section heading, on every page and the draft;
  - the tile at the top of every page;
  - the icons on the "where next" cards;
  - the mark before every list line. Lists become plain text with a little space between lines, and ordinary bullets where a list needs them.
- **Unused icons removed from `src/lib/icons.ts`,** so nothing is shipped that isn't shown.
- **Built on `preview`,** and published to `cfgaming.app` with phase 04's pictures, on Marc's say.

## The checks

- `zsh .claude/skills/phase-report/checks.zsh` clean.
- The pages on the preview, at a phone's width and a desktop's, dark and light: headings still read as headings without their tiles, and lists still read as lists.
- The site-style reviewer, with the house style's "icon tiles" read against Marc's "sparingly".
- Marc looks at the preview.
