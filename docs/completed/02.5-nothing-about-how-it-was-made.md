## What every page says now (read 14 November, on `preview` and `main`)

- **About how things were made:**
  - every page's footer: "The logo comes from Charddie Games, on YouTube.";
  - Licences, "This website": "Built with Astro, under the MIT licence." No code of Astro's reaches the browser (the built site has no JavaScript), so its licence asks for nothing on the site;
  - `main`'s About: "CF Gaming's logo is the one I made for their YouTube channel". `preview` already has Marc's own words there instead.
- **The trademark notice,** which isn't about how anything was made: in every page's footer, and a second time on Licences.
- **Kept, as licences require them:** the Licences page's credits and source links (Wine under the LGPL with CodeWeavers' source and the app's patches, Mupen64Plus-Next under the GPL with its source, D3DMetal, DXMT, MoltenVK and the rest), and the app's own credits that it repeats.
- **Kept, as they say what CF Gaming does:** Home's "made from CodeWeavers' open source", Install's Developer ID and notarisation, Privacy's Cloudflare and GitHub.
- **Not on the site:** `CREDITS.md` in the repository keeps how each picture was made, and code comments say why the banner is never wider than 1280 pixels.

## Done (14 November 2026)

### What it does now

- **Nothing on the site says how something was made.** The footer no longer says where the logo came from, and Licences no longer says what the site is built with.
- **Every page's footer:** the site's pages, and a link, "Charddie Games, on YouTube". The trademark notice is gone from it.
- **Licences** ends with the trademark notice, once. Its credits and source links, which the licences require, are as they were.
- **About:**
  - "My sons wanted to play their Windows games on their Macs, DirectX 12 ones included. CodeWeavers' CrossOver could do it, but it cost more than we wanted to spend."
  - "So I built CF Gaming for the latest macOS: its own Wine engine, made from the same open source CrossOver is built on, with Apple's D3DMetal for DirectX 12, and Steam in a case of its own, everything set up for them."
  - "It's free for now. Soon there'll be a cost to it, but if you install it now, it stays free for you for good. Well done for finding it early."
  - The banner is the link to the channel; the button is gone.
- **"It was expensive" became "it cost more than we wanted to spend":** the same meaning, as the family's own experience, not a judgement of a named company whose source CF Gaming uses.
- **Going live:** with this phase's commit, `main` is fast-forwarded to `preview`, and Marc pushes `main`.
