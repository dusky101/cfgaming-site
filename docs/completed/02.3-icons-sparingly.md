# 02.3: icons, sparingly

Fixes after Marc's look at the site, 14 November 2026. Agree it with Marc before it starts.

## Why, in Marc's words

"Also remove the icons from the start of every line. if ever there was a way to tell if a website was built by ai that is it.. 🙂 Ai loves the icons..." Then: "use icons still but sparingly."

## What's there now (counted 14 November)

- **A tile before every section heading:** 32 across the pages (`.with-tile`), and 4 more on the DirectX 12 draft.
- **A tile at the top of every page,** in `PageHeader.astro`: 7.
- **An icon on each "where next" card,** in `NextSteps.astro`.
- **A gradient mark before every line of every list** (`.ticks li::before` in `global.css`): the marks at "the start of every line".
- **The download icon** on the download buttons.

## The proposal

- **Kept:**
  - **the download icon** on the download buttons: it says what the button does;
  - **one icon for each of Home's three highlights** (Windows games, consoles, Custom Setup), until phase 04's pictures take their place;
  - **Install's numbered steps** (1 to 4): they're numbers, not icons.
- **Gone:**
  - the tile before every section heading, on every page and the draft;
  - the tile at the top of every page;
  - the icons on the "where next" cards;
  - the mark before every list line. Lists become plain text with a little space between lines, and ordinary bullets where a list needs them.
- **Unused icons removed from `src/lib/icons.ts`,** so nothing is shipped that isn't shown.
- **Built on `preview`,** and published to `cfgaming.app` with phase 04's pictures, on Marc's say.

## The checks

- `zsh .claude/skills/phase-report/checks.zsh` clean.
- The pages on the preview, at a phone's width and a desktop's, dark and light: headings still read as headings without their tiles, and lists still read as lists.
- The site-style reviewer, with the house style's "icon tiles" read against Marc's "sparingly".
- Marc looks at the preview.

## For Marc

1. **The three icons on Home's highlights:** keep them until the pictures arrive, or remove them now as well?

## Done (14 November 2026)

Marc agreed the proposal on 14 November and kept Home's icons: "Yes, keep Home's three".

### What it does now

- **One icon on most pages:** the download button's arrow, in the header. Home also has the arrows on its two big download buttons, and an icon on each of its three highlights. Install keeps its numbered steps (1 to 4), and so does the DirectX 12 draft.
- **Headings stand alone:** no tile before any section heading, and none above any page's title.
- **"Where next" cards:** the page's name and its line, with no icon.
- **Lists:** ordinary bullets, a little apart, in place of a coloured dot before every line.
- **Home's chips** ("Free", "Macs with Apple silicon", "macOS 27 or later"): text only. They weren't in the count above, and they weren't on the list of icons to keep, so their icons went too.
- **Built on `preview`.** It goes live with phase 04's pictures, on Marc's say (phase 04, step 9).

### How it's built

| File | Change |
|---|---|
| `src/pages/*.astro`, `src/drafts/directx-12.astro` | Each heading's tile and its wrapper removed, keeping the heading and its id. Lists use `.points` with ordinary bullets, without `role="list"`, which they no longer need. Unused `Icon` imports removed. |
| `src/pages/index.astro` | The tiles on "And more" and the chips' icons removed. The three highlights keep theirs. The first highlight's three columns now use `.points`. |
| `src/components/PageHeader.astro` | The tile and the `icon` prop removed. |
| `src/components/NextSteps.astro` | The card icons removed. The title is a block, with a small gap below it. |
| `src/lib/site.ts` | `icon` removed from each page. A stale comment corrected. |
| `src/lib/icons.ts` | Down from 26 drawings to the 4 still shown: download, monitor, gamepad and sliders. |
| `src/styles/global.css` | `.ticks` became `.points`. `.tile-soft`, `.chip svg`, the chip's gap and the forced-colours dot rule were removed. `.tile`, `.tile-large`, `.tile-number` and `.with-tile` stay, with comments saying where they're used. |

On this Mac only, never in git: `.claude/rules/house-style-web.md` now says "icon tiles, sparingly" with Marc's words and the list kept. The site-style reviewer reads it that way.
