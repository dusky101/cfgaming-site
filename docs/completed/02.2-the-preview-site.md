## The aim

Changes show first on a preview of their own on Cloudflare, and reach `cfgaming.app` only when Marc says the preview is right.
- **`preview`** is the branch the work happens on. Cloudflare builds it as a Preview.
- **`main`** is the live site, and stays as it is until then.

## What it starts from

- **Hosting:** the site is the `cfgaming-site` Worker, built by Workers Builds at each push to `main` (phase 02.1).
- **Version addresses:** `wrangler.jsonc` sets `preview_urls` to `false`, which turns them off. That's why `https://<version>-cfgaming-site.<subdomain>.workers.dev` showed "There is nothing here yet".
- **Cloudflare's docs (read 14 November), proved:**
  - builds for other branches are switched on at Settings › Build › Branch control › **Enable Preview Builds**;
  - their command, `npx wrangler preview`, makes a Preview that "always serves the latest deployment" at `<preview-name>-cfgaming-site.<subdomain>.workers.dev`, with `X-Robots-Tag: noindex`;
  - Previews are public unless Cloudflare Access is put in front of them;
  - Workers connected before Previews existed may offer a one-time switch to them, which "cannot be reversed".
- **The docs don't say:**
  - whether a Preview's name is the branch's name;
  - whether `preview_urls: false` affects Previews;
  - whether a Worker with static assets only gets them;
  - what Access costs.
- **Set up before the restart, on this Mac only:**
  - `.claude/rules/cloudflare.md`, with the two branches and what may be changed in Cloudflare;
  - `CLAUDE.md`;
  - `.claude/hooks/guard_bash.py`: no commits on `main`, and nothing merged into it but `git merge --ff-only preview`;
  - `.claude/hooks/guard_cloudflare.py`: the irreversible Cloudflare calls refused;
  - `settings.json`: every Cloudflare plugin call asks Marc;
  - the two skills.
  - Work is now on the `preview` branch.
