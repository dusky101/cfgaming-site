# About this site

A first draft (13 November 2026), from what Marc has said; his to change. The site's About page tells the same story to visitors, in his own words.

## Why CF Gaming exists

- **It began at home.** Marc's sons wanted to play their Windows games, DirectX 12 ones included, on their Macs. Nothing they tried did all of it simply, so Marc built CF Gaming: its own Wine engine, from CodeWeavers' open source, with Apple's D3DMetal for DirectX 12, Steam in a "case" of its own, and everything set up for them.
- **Then the consoles.** Once Windows games played, the old consoles followed: RetroArch and its cores, set up by CF Gaming, with each console's controller, memory cards, cheats, texture packs and its own settings for every game. Mac games and other emulators sit beside them.
- **One place for every game on the Mac**, set up for a family: nothing to configure by hand, calm words, and help built in.

## What the site is for

- **To show CF Gaming as it is**, with pictures from the app itself, and say plainly what it does and what it doesn't do yet.
- **To download it in one click**, straight from its latest release, with what's needed beforehand and how to install it.
- **To explain it:** before you start, installing, the guide, the licences and the source.
- **To be the best site of its kind:** modern, fast, accessible, and as carefully made as the app.

## What it promises

- **CF Gaming is free.** Marc may ask for a small one-time payment one day; the site would say so plainly first.
- **No adverts, no tracking, no cookies,** and nothing from other companies loaded into a visitor's browser.
- **Honest:** only what the app does now, with its known issues, never "coming soon" without Marc's say.
- **Fair to everyone whose work it builds on:** every licence and credit, Wine's source changes, no one else's logos or artwork.

## Who it's for

- People with a Mac with Apple silicon who want to play their Windows and console games, families first.
- Marc's own family, whose story it is, though their names and anything private stay off the site unless Marc says.
